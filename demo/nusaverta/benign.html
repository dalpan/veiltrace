Export invoices to CSV

Updated 3 days ago · 4 min read

You can export any list of invoices to a CSV file for your accountant or for import into a spreadsheet.

Export from the invoice list

  1. Open Invoices and filter the list by date, customer or status.
  2. Select Export in the top-right corner of the list.
  3. Choose CSV and the columns you want to include.
  4. Select Download. Large exports are emailed to you when they are ready.

Exports include only invoices you are allowed to see. Ask a workspace admin if some invoices are missing.

Columns in the export

ColumnDescription
invoice_numberYour invoice number, for example INV-2026-0142
issue_dateDate the invoice was issued (YYYY-MM-DD)
customerCustomer name as shown on the invoice
totalInvoice total including tax
statusDraft, sent, paid or overdue
