Websites not loading on campus Wi-Fi after the network change (KB0412)

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Applies to: staff and student Windows laptops

After the October 12 move to the new campus DNS servers, some Windows laptops keep old addresses in their cache. Symptoms: the portal and library sites time out while other sites work.

Fix: clear the DNS cache

  1. Press Windows + R, type cmd and press Enter.
  2. In the Command Prompt window, type the following and press Enter:
    ipconfig /flushdns
  3. You should see "Successfully flushed the DNS Resolver Cache." Close the window and reload the page.

If the problem continues, open a ticket from the Service Portal and include your laptop's asset tag. Please do not run commands sent to you by email or chat that are not from this knowledge base.
