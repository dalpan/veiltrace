How we'll contact you, and how we won't

Fraudsters often pretend to be bank staff. Knowing what we never do is the best protection.

We will never

  • Ask you to install screen-sharing or remote-access apps
  • Ask for your full PIN, password or one-time passcodes
  • Ask you to move money to a "safe account"
  • Pressure you to act within minutes

If you're unsure

Hang up and call us back using the number on the back of your card or in our mobile app. Wait a minute before dialling so the line clears.
