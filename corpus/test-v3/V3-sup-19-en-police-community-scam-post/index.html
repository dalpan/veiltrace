Residents report calls from fake "detectives"

Posted Oct 18, 2026 · Community Safety Unit

Several residents received calls claiming their identity was linked to a crime and that they must pay a "bond" in gift cards or crypto to avoid arrest, and keep the call secret from family.

We will never demand payment by phone, gift card or cryptocurrency, and we never ask you to keep an investigation secret from your family. Hang up and call our non-emergency line listed on this website.
