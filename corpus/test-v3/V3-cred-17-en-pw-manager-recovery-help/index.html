Save your Emergency Recovery Kit

Your recovery kit contains your secret key. You need it to sign in on a new device.

  1. In the app, open Settings > Security > Recovery Kit.
  2. Click Download PDF and print it.
  3. Store the paper copy somewhere safe, such as a home safe.

Important: VaultKeep support will never ask for your secret key or master password. Never type them into any website other than the VaultKeep app, and never share them by chat or phone.
