"Just pay the tax and it unlocks": how crypto romance scams drain savings

By R. Patel · Oct 19, 2026

For months, retired nurse Linda chatted daily with a man she met online. Her trading dashboard showed big gains. When she tried to withdraw, he wrote: you just need to pay the profit tax first, then everything unlocks.

Investigators say no real exchange charges a fee to release your own funds. "If a platform asks you to pay to withdraw, stop and talk to your bank," one fraud officer said.
