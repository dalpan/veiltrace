VeilTrace helps uncover threats hidden behind digital content. With context-aware analysis that puts privacy first, VeilTrace detects signs of fraud, social engineering and manipulation, and explains the risk behind them.

VeilTrace membantu mengungkap ancaman yang tersembunyi di balik konten digital. Dengan analisis kontekstual yang mengutamakan privasi, VeilTrace mendeteksi indikasi penipuan, social engineering, dan manipulasi serta menjelaskan risiko di baliknya.

How it worksCara kerja

01

Read-only extraction

Reads the visible text and where it comes from: comments, widgets, dialogs or chat. Form contents are never read.

Membaca teks yang terlihat dan asal kontennya: komentar, widget, dialog, atau chat. Isi form tidak pernah dibaca.

02

Contextual correlation

Combines the requested action (run, send a code, pay, contact) with a manipulative pretext (claimed authority, time pressure, a promised prize).

Menggabungkan action yang diminta (jalankan, kirim kode, bayar, hubungi) dengan pretext manipulatif (klaim otoritas, desakan waktu, iming-iming hadiah).

03

Explainable evidence

Every finding comes with its reason and evidence that can be highlighted on the page. A single keyword never triggers a warning.

Setiap temuan punya alasan dan bukti yang bisa di-highlight langsung di halaman. Satu keyword saja tidak pernah memicu peringatan.

Four detectorsEmpat detector

CMD

Command Execution

Fake CAPTCHAs ("press Win+R, Ctrl+V, Enter", ClickFix) and "fix this error" steps in a terminal.

CAPTCHA palsu "tekan Win+R, Ctrl+V, Enter" (ClickFix), "perbaiki error" lewat terminal.

CRED

Credential Disclosure

Requests for OTP codes, passwords or recovery phrases, including in slang.

Permintaan kode OTP, password, atau recovery phrase, termasuk dalam bahasa gaul.

PAY

Payment Manipulation

Pay first to release a refund or prize, transfers to personal accounts, job-offer uniform fees, gift cards.

Bayar dulu agar refund atau hadiah cair, transfer ke rekening pribadi, biaya seragam lowongan, gift card.

SUP

Support & Authority Impersonation

Fake virus alerts, a "bank CS" asking you to install AnyDesk or an APK, fake tariff notices, prize quizzes, phone-tapping services.

Fake virus alert, "CS bank" meminta AnyDesk/APK, notifikasi tarif palsu, kuis berhadiah, jasa sadap.

  • Only two permissions: activeTab and scripting. Auto-scan asks for more only when you turn it on.
  • Hanya dua permission: activeTab dan scripting. Auto-scan meminta izin tambahan hanya saat Anda menyalakannya.

Chrome / Edge

  1. Download veiltrace-chromium.zip and extract it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
  5. Pin the VeilTrace icon, open a page, then click Scan.

  3. Aktifkan Developer mode.

FAQ

Does VeilTrace send my data to the internet?

No. Every check runs on your device. The extension and the app contain no network code, and this is tested automatically.

Does VeilTrace guarantee that a page can be trusted?

No. VeilTrace reports what it found, with evidence. When there are no findings, it means no known manipulation pattern was recognized, not that the page is guaranteed.

Do I have to click Scan every time?

Manual by default, for privacy. You can turn on auto-scan from the popup: pages are checked when they load, and the icon is marked when manipulative content is found.

Why not use AI?

We tested on-device AI. It flagged ordinary pages far too often, its reasons could not be checked, and text on the page can talk it into a different answer. VeilTrace uses explainable rules: every warning comes with evidence.

How much does it cost?

Free and open source (Apache-2.0).

Apakah VeilTrace mengirim data saya ke internet?

Tidak. Semua pemeriksaan berjalan di perangkat Anda. Extension dan app tidak punya network code; ini diuji otomatis.

Apakah VeilTrace menjamin sebuah halaman bisa dipercaya?

Tidak. VeilTrace melaporkan apa yang ditemukan beserta buktinya. Bila tidak ada temuan, artinya tidak ada pola manipulasi yang dikenali, bukan jaminan.

Harus klik Scan setiap kali?

Bawaannya manual demi privasi. Anda bisa menyalakan auto-scan dari popup: halaman diperiksa saat dimuat dan ikon diberi tanda bila ada konten manipulatif.

Kenapa tidak memakai AI?

Kami sudah menguji AI on-device. AI terlalu sering menandai halaman wajar, alasannya tidak bisa diperiksa, dan bisa dibujuk oleh teks di halaman. VeilTrace memakai rule yang bisa dijelaskan: setiap peringatan punya bukti.

Berapa harganya?

Gratis dan open source (Apache-2.0).
